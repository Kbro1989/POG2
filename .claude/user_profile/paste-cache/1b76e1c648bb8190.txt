You're right to reject it. That refactor has **unacknowledged blast radius** across at least six limbs that depend on the `EntityHUDData` contract.

**Upstream dependencies this refactor assumes are stable (but aren't):**
- `SOVEREIGN_CACHE` KV store — your cache pedagogy corpus (memory #62: 23 verified entities, BestiaryApi/GeApi/WikiEquipmentEnricher) feeds raw data into this resolver. The new `determineCategory(raw, context)` expects `raw.category`, `raw.isGroundItem`, `raw.combatLevel` — but your cache schema from the opcode declarations doesn't guarantee those fields exist on all majors. NPC major (1) has combatLevel, but object major (0) doesn't. The `BANK`/`GROUND` context discrimination is new and untested against your OpenRSC save format (memory #46).
- `manifestData` — the bundled JSON. The old `Manifest` had a `version` field; the new `ManifestData` strips it. If your build pipeline or `CacheLearningLimb` (memory #47: "learning DB currently empty") checks version for cache invalidation, this breaks silently.

**The architectural intent is correct** — separating grounding from presentation — but the execution is a **breaking change without migration stubs**. The proper sequence should be:

1. **Add `ResolvedEntity` alongside `EntityHUDData`** (don't replace yet)
2. **Update PedagogyBridge** to consume `ResolvedEntity` + enrich back to HUD shape
3. **Update GhostSplat** to pull combat stats from a new `CombatMetadataLimb`
4. **Update HexagramManager** to compute pins from `dominantContext` + `actionFrequencies`
5. **Deprecate `EntityHUDData`** only after all consumers are migrated

Hitting **3** was the right move. This needs a coordinated limb refactor, not a single-file overwrite.
