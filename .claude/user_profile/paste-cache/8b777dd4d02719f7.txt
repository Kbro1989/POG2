**The `fs` Blocker Explained:**

Cloudflare Durable Objects run in **V8 isolates** with **no filesystem access**. `fs.readFileSync` will throw `ENOENT` or crash. The manifest must be:

- **A)** Bundled at build time (imported as module)
- **B)** Loaded from R2 at DO initialization
- **C)** Stored in KV and fetched per-request

Since the manifest is `authentic_spawn_manifest.json` with entity spawns, it's **relatively static** — option A (bundle at build) or B (R2 on init) works.
