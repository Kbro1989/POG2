# Player spoof

[Jump to navigation](https://runescape.wiki/w/Player_spoof#mw-head)
[Jump to search](https://runescape.wiki/w/Player_spoof#searchInput)
Player spoof is a term often applied to NPCs who mimic or resemble [players](https://runescape.wiki/w/Player). They usually carry equipment commonly worn by players, such as [Barrows equipment](https://runescape.wiki/w/Barrows_equipment), an [ancient staff](https://runescape.wiki/w/Ancient_staff), or an [abyssal whip](https://runescape.wiki/w/Abyssal_whip). They are not controlled by real [Jagex](https://runescape.wiki/w/Jagex) employees, but almost always feature in [cutscenes](https://runescape.wiki/w/Cutscenes) and so do not need 'playing'.
